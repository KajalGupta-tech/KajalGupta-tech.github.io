about

The person behind the pipelines

I'm a data analyst who likes messy datasets — the kind with nulls, duplicates, and outliers that most people avoid. That's usually where the real story starts. I studied Computer Science (Honours) at the University of Delhi, and along the way built a habit of asking "so what does this number actually mean for the business?" before touching a single chart.

My work sits at the intersection of statistics, engineering, and storytelling — cleaning and modeling data in Python, querying it in SQL, and shipping the insight as a dashboard someone will actually open on a Monday morning.

Recently I completed a virtual internship with Deloitte Australia (via Forage), applying data visualization and modeling skills to real business scenarios — and I'm now looking for a full-time seat at that table.
